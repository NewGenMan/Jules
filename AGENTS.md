# System Principles - Jim Simons Approach

The following principles must be adhered to in this project:

1. **Purely Systematic Execution**: Never override the computer. Models must operate without human emotion or gut feelings.
2. **Statistical Arbitrage & Mean Reversion**: Focus on statistical anomalies and the tendency for asset prices to return to their mean.
3. **High-Frequency, Small Edges**: Seek to be right slightly more than 50% of the time. Profit through volume and consistent execution.
4. **Dynamic Evolution**: Constantly test and refine models as financial anomalies disappear.
